1. Why Native Phone Trackers Often Fail in Theft Situations

Most smartphone owners believe that Apple’s Find My iPhone or Google’s Find My Device will guarantee recovery. However, in reality, theft scenarios break native utilities in 3 critical ways:

  • Two-Factor SMS Lockout: When you try to log into iCloud or your Google account from a computer, Apple or Google sends a verification SMS code to your phone—which is in the thief's possession.
  • Airplane Mode & Immediate Power Off: Thieves immediately swipe down the control center to enable Airplane Mode or shut the battery down, cutting all data connections.
  • SIM Card Ejection: Removing the SIM card severs cellular data, preventing native apps from transmitting their coordinates over the internet.
