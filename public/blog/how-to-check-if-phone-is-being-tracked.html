1. How to Test Your Line with GSM MMI Diagnostic Codes

Before suspecting advanced network surveillance, check whether your cellular carrier has active call or SMS diverts enabled on your SIM card. Open your phone’s regular dialer app and test the following standardized GSM codes:

*#21# Unconditional Diversion

Displays whether all incoming voice calls, SMS messages, or packet data are automatically forwarded to another phone number. Every line should read "Disabled" or "Not forwarded".

*#62# No-Reply Diversion

Checks where calls or data are routed when your device is unreachable or powered down. Normally, this displays your mobile carrier’s official voicemail center number.

##002# Master Reset Code

Universal cancel command that instantly clears all active call forwardings and redirections on your mobile line.

2. Software Spyware vs. Carrier Network Telemetry

It is critical to distinguish between application-level spyware and carrier network tracking:

Local App Spyware

Requires physical installation or phishing downloads. Manifests as sudden battery drain, abnormal data usage spikes, and unfamiliar background apps in settings.

Carrier Network Geolocation

Operates at the telecommunications provider level. Uses serving cell tower timing advance and SS7 routing to resolve physical coordinates without installing anything on the handset.
