# Antigravity Workspace Guidelines

## Core Principles
1. **Never Look Like AI ("Maybanch AI"):**
   - Strictly prohibit generic sci-fi purples, robotic buzzwords, "AI-powered", fake futuristic grids, or artificial corporate clichés.
   - Design websites to look 100% like reputable, high-converting commercial platforms crafted by elite human engineers.
   - Use authentic telecom, engineering, or industry-specific terminology.

2. **Design Skills Activation:**
   - Whenever working on frontend, UI, UX, styling, or layouts, systematically apply the principles from:
     - `ui-ux-pro-max`: 160+ UX reasoning rules, 60-30-10 palette balance, hierarchy, responsive behavior.
     - `frontend-design`: Craftsmanship, micro-interactions, tactile polish, typography, and anti-AI aesthetics.

3. **Design System & Aesthetics:**
   - **Colors:** 60-30-10 distribution rule. Neutral foundations (`#0c1524`, `#f8fafc`, `#1e293b`) with deliberate accent colors (e.g. `#10b981` emerald green).
   - **Typography:** Clear hierarchy, modern human sans-serif typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`).
   - **Micro-interactions:** Smooth cubic-bezier transitions, tactile buttons, high-accuracy data metrics, and verified human social proof.
