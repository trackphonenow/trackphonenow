1. Why Standard Screenshots Aren't Enough for Official Verification

Insurance providers, corporate IT asset managers, and mobile carriers process thousands of lost phone claims every week. Standard consumer screenshots can be easily cropped or spoofed, and often fail to satisfy formal verification requirements.

To prove a device's last active location, formal claims require Verifiable Telemetry backed by technical cellular records:

  • Fixed Latitude and Longitude Coordinates: Down to 6 decimal places (e.g., 40.712776, -74.005974) with confidence radius.
  • Reverse-Geocoded Street Address: Verified physical building or neighborhood location.
  • Cell ID & BTS Tower Vector: Identification of the physical cellular transceiver serving the handset.
  • Cryptographic Timestamps: Exact UTC seconds of the last radio handshake and SHA-256 verification hash.

Having verified timestamps and coordinates accelerates claim processing and establishes indisputable proof of device location. Keep digital copies of your hardware IMEI number along with the exported PDF dossier for complete records.
